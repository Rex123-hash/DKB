# MUDRA loan categories and limits (updated 2024-25)

The Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana gives collateral-free loans to small, non-farm businesses through banks, NBFCs and microfinance institutions. It is meant for activities like manufacturing, trading, services and shopkeeping.

There are now four categories. Shishu covers loans up to ₹50,000. Kishore covers ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh. Tarun covers above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh.

A new category, Tarun Plus, was added and covers ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh. It is for borrowers who have already taken and fully repaid a Tarun loan. This higher ₹20 lakh limit was announced in the Union Budget 2024-25 and took effect from 24 October 2024.

MUDRA loans are collateral-free, which makes them suitable for a shopkeeper who needs working capital or money to expand but has nothing to pledge. A clean repayment record helps you graduate to the larger categories.

To apply, approach a bank or lending institution with your business details and KYC. Good bookkeeping and a bank statement that shows steady turnover strengthen the application.

Source: MUDRA (mudra.org.in), Press Information Bureau, Union Budget 2024-25.
