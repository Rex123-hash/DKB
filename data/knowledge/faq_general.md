# Using Dukanbook and general shop questions — FAQ

Q: What can the Dukanbook AI assistant do?
A: It manages your udhaar khata (credits and debits for customers and suppliers), answers accounting, GST, tax and business questions, and sets payment and call reminders — by text or voice, in Hindi, English or Hinglish.

Q: How do I record udhaar with the assistant?
A: Just say or type it naturally, like "Ramesh ko 500 udhaar likho". The assistant records the credit and tells you the new balance. To record a payment received, say "Ramesh ne 200 jama kiye".

Q: How do I check how much someone owes?
A: Ask "Ramesh kitna baaki hai?" or "Ramesh ka balance?". The assistant reads it from your ledger and tells you the outstanding amount.

Q: Can it set payment reminders?
A: Yes. Say "Ramesh ko kal subah 10 baje payment ke liye yaad dilana" and it schedules a reminder, which appears in the Reminders section.

Q: What languages does it understand?
A: Hindi, English and Hinglish (mixed). You can speak or type the way you normally talk, and it replies in the same style.

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Your ledger is stored in the app's database, and the knowledge answers come from a built-in reference base. Protect access with a screen lock and never share OTPs or PINs.

Q: What is the difference between credit and debit in my khata?
A: A credit means you gave goods or money on udhaar, so the party owes you more. A debit means they paid back or you received money, reducing what they owe. Balance is credits minus debits.

Q: Can the assistant tell me the weather or do maths?
A: Yes — it can give the current weather for a city and do basic calculations. For live cricket scores it will tell you it does not have that yet.

Q: What should I do every day in my shop?
A: Record each sale and udhaar entry the same day, confirm digital payments before giving goods, count the cash against your records at closing, and note any stock running low.

Q: How does the assistant help me grow?
A: By keeping your accounts accurate, reminding customers to pay, answering your GST and business questions, and letting you review your sales and outstanding dues quickly so you make better decisions.
