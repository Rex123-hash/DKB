# PM SVANidhi scheme for street vendors

PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) gives small, collateral-free working-capital loans to street vendors, so they can restart or grow their business.

The loan is given in steps. The first loan is ₹10,000 (raised to ₹15,000 under the updated scheme). After full repayment, the vendor becomes eligible for a second loan of ₹20,000 (raised to ₹25,000), and after repaying that, a third loan of ₹50,000 under PM SVANidhi 2.0. Each step is unlocked by repaying the previous one on time.

On regular, timely repayment the vendor gets an interest subsidy of 7% per year. The scheme also rewards digital transactions with a cashback of up to ₹1,200 a year, which encourages vendors to accept UPI and card payments.

To be eligible, a street vendor needs a certificate of vending or identity card from the Urban Local Body, or a provisional certificate if surveyed, or a Letter of Recommendation from the Town Vending Committee or Urban Local Body.

The scheme is useful for very small vendors who cannot get a normal bank loan, and it also pushes them towards digital payments and a formal credit record.

Source: PM SVANidhi, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in). Amounts and rules may be revised, so verify on the official portal.
