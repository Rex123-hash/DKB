# Licenses and compliance for shops

The Shop and Establishment Act registration, often called a Gumasta licence, is the basic permission to run a shop or commercial establishment, issued by the state or municipal authority. It records your business details and working hours and is frequently needed to open a current account or apply for other licences.

An FSSAI registration or licence is required for any business that handles food — grocery, sweets, restaurants, packaged food. The level (basic registration, state licence, or central licence) depends on turnover and scale. The FSSAI number printed on bills and packaging assures customers the food is from a registered seller.

A trade licence from the local municipal body permits you to carry on a particular trade at a particular location. It ensures the business follows local health and safety rules. Requirements vary by city, so check with your municipal corporation for your specific trade.

GST registration is itself a compliance requirement once your turnover crosses the threshold, or immediately if you sell across state lines or online. Even below the threshold, voluntary registration can help you claim input tax credit and deal with registered buyers.

Udyam (MSME) registration, though not compulsory, is a valuable, free online step that unlocks cheaper loans, government tender benefits, and legal protection against delayed payments. Keeping your Udyam details updated as the business grows preserves these benefits.

Renew licences before they expire and keep copies — physical and digital — readily available. Lapsed registrations can mean fines, and many banks and suppliers ask to see current licences. A simple file or folder of all your registrations saves stress during inspections or loan applications.

Display the required registrations where customers and inspectors can see them, such as the GSTIN on invoices and the FSSAI number on food bills. Visible compliance builds customer trust and signals that yours is a serious, lawful business.

Different businesses need different permissions — for example a pharmacy needs a drug licence, and some trades need a weights-and-measures verification for their scales. Identify the specific licences for your line of business early, so growth is never held back by a missing permit.
