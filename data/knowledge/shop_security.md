# Shop security and risk safety

Protect your cash by banking takings regularly rather than keeping large amounts on the premises, and by encouraging digital payments that need no cash drawer. Less cash on hand means less to lose to theft or robbery.

Guard against fake currency by checking high notes, and prefer UPI and card payments which cannot be faked. Train staff to verify any "payment done" claim against the actual bank credit before handing over goods.

Beware common scams: fake payment screenshots, requests to scan a QR to "receive" money (scanning only sends money), distraction thefts in busy moments, and callers asking for OTPs or UPI PINs. Never share an OTP or PIN with anyone.

Insure the shop against fire, theft and natural damage. A modest shop insurance policy protects years of investment in stock and fittings from a single bad event, and some policies also cover loss of cash in transit to the bank.

Secure the premises physically — good locks, shutters, lighting, and a camera where affordable. Visible security discourages theft, and a camera helps resolve disputes and identify culprits.

Keep digital security tight: lock your phone and business apps with a strong PIN, keep software updated, and never click suspicious links or share banking credentials. Your accounts and customer data are as valuable as the cash box.

Back up your records. A cloud-backed digital khata survives a lost or damaged phone, while a paper register can be destroyed in one accident. Regular backup is cheap insurance for your most important business asset — its records.

Plan for emergencies: keep a small cash buffer, know your supplier and bank contacts, and keep copies of key licences and documents safe. Being prepared turns a crisis into an inconvenience rather than a disaster.
