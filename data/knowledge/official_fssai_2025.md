# FSSAI registration and licence levels

Any business that handles food needs an FSSAI registration or licence, and the level depends on annual turnover. This covers grocery shops, sweet shops, restaurants, home bakers, food stalls and packaged-food sellers.

A food business with annual turnover below ₹12 lakh needs the basic FSSAI registration, applied for using Form A. This suits small vendors, home bakers, food stalls and small traders.

A business with turnover from ₹12 lakh up to ₹20 crore that operates within a single state needs the FSSAI state licence, applied for using Form B. This suits restaurants, cafes, mid-size processors and single-state caterers.

A business with turnover above ₹20 crore, or one that operates in more than one state or does import or export, needs the FSSAI central licence.

The FSSAI number should be printed on bills and food packaging. It assures customers the food is from a registered seller and is required by law for food businesses, so getting the right level early avoids penalties.

Source: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (fssai.gov.in). Turnover slabs can change, so verify on the official portal.
