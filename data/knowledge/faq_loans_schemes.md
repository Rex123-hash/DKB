# Loans and government schemes — frequently asked questions

Q: What is the MUDRA loan scheme?
A: A government scheme giving collateral-free loans to small non-farm businesses in three categories — Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000–₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5 lakh–₹10 lakh) — through banks and finance companies.

Q: What is PM SVANidhi?
A: A scheme for street vendors offering a small collateral-free working-capital loan, starting around ₹10,000, with larger amounts on timely repayment and incentives for digital transactions.

Q: What is Udyam registration and why get it?
A: It is the free online MSME registration. An Udyam number helps you get collateral-free loans, lower interest, government-tender benefits and legal protection against delayed payments. Most small shops qualify as micro enterprises.

Q: What is a CIBIL score and what is a good one?
A: A credit score from 300 to 900 reflecting how reliably you repay. Above about 750 is good and helps you get loans faster and cheaper. Paying EMIs and bills on time improves it.

Q: What is working capital and how do I fund it?
A: It is the cash needed for day-to-day running — stock, rent, wages — before sales come in. A working-capital loan or a current-account overdraft can bridge the gap, repaid as sales flow in.

Q: Should I borrow from a moneylender?
A: Avoid informal high-interest lenders where possible; their rates can trap a small shop in debt. Banks, registered NBFCs and government schemes offer far lower rates.

Q: What should I check before taking a loan?
A: Compare the interest rate, processing fees and total repayment, and make sure the EMI fits your cash flow. Borrow for things that grow the business — stock, equipment, a better location — not routine expenses.

Q: How does good bookkeeping help me get loans?
A: Clean accounts, a filed income-tax return and a steady bank statement are exactly what lenders look for. Good records, especially digital payment history, directly improve access to affordable finance.

Q: Are these loans collateral-free?
A: MUDRA and PM SVANidhi loans are designed to be collateral-free. An Udyam registration further improves your eligibility for collateral-free credit from banks.
