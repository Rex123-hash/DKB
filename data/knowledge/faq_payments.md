# Payments and banking — frequently asked questions

Q: How does a UPI QR code help my shop?
A: Customers scan it and pay instantly from their phone to your bank account. It is free, settles in seconds, reduces cash handling and fake-note risk, and records every payment automatically for your books.

Q: If I scan a customer's QR code, do I receive money?
A: No. Scanning a QR only sends money; it never receives. If someone asks you to scan a QR to "receive" a payment, it is a scam — you would be paying them.

Q: Should I use a current account or a savings account for the shop?
A: A current account, kept separate from personal money. It suits frequent transactions, supports higher limits, links to a payment QR and card machine, and keeps records clean for loans and tax.

Q: What is the difference between NEFT, RTGS and IMPS?
A: NEFT settles in batches and suits routine payments; RTGS is for large amounts in real time; IMPS is instant and works 24x7 including holidays. For small payments, UPI or IMPS is usually fastest.

Q: How do I avoid payment fraud?
A: Confirm money has actually reached your bank account before handing over goods — do not trust screenshots. Never share OTPs or UPI PINs, and remember scanning a QR only sends money.

Q: Are card payments worth accepting?
A: Yes — accepting cards and UPI means you rarely lose a sale for lack of cash. Cards carry a small MDR fee, while UPI is generally free, so encourage UPI where possible.

Q: What is a bounced cheque?
A: A cheque that fails because the account lacks funds. It is a legal offence and delays your payment. Digital payments are more reliable and faster for most shop dealings.

Q: How should I match payments with my records?
A: Every UPI and card payment should appear in both your shop record and your bank statement. Matching them regularly catches missed entries, failed payments and unauthorised charges early.

Q: Can digital payments help me get a loan?
A: Yes. A steady stream of digital receipts gives banks a clear, verifiable picture of your turnover, which strengthens loan applications and can lower interest rates.

Q: Is there a charge to customers for UPI payments?
A: No, UPI is free for customers paying from their bank account. This is why displaying a UPI QR rarely discourages a sale and speeds up collection.
