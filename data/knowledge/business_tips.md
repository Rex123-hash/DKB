# Practical business tips for local shopkeepers

Recovering udhaar (dues) works best with gentle, consistent reminders. Send a polite message a few days before the due date, again on the due date, and a friendly follow-up after. Most customers pay late simply because they forget, so a timely reminder recovers money without spoiling the relationship.

Set a credit limit for each customer. Decide a maximum amount you are willing to give on udhaar based on how well you know them and their payment history. When a customer reaches their limit, ask for part payment before giving more. This prevents one customer's unpaid balance from growing too large to recover.

Track which customers pay on time and which do not. Reward reliable customers with small discounts or priority, and be more careful with slow payers. Your ledger data shows this pattern clearly if you record every transaction.

Manage stock so you neither run out nor over-buy. Watch which items sell fast and reorder them before they finish, and avoid tying up cash in slow-moving goods. A simple low-stock reminder helps you reorder at the right time, especially for daily essentials.

Plan for festival and seasonal demand in advance. Sales of sweets, gifts, clothes and decorations rise sharply around festivals like Diwali, Holi, Eid and Raksha Bandhan. Order extra stock and arrange cash a few weeks early so you do not miss the peak selling days.

Keep a small cash buffer for the business. Aim to set aside enough to cover one to two months of rent and regular expenses. This buffer protects the shop during slow weeks and lets you buy stock at a good price when an opportunity comes.

Accept digital payments alongside cash. UPI and QR-code payments are fast, leave an automatic record, and many customers prefer them. The digital record also makes your bookkeeping and tax filing easier because every payment is already logged.

Review your numbers once a week. Spend a few minutes checking total sales, total udhaar outstanding, and which bills are due. A short weekly review catches problems early and helps you make better buying and credit decisions.
