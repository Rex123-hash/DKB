# Licenses and compliance — frequently asked questions

Q: What is a Gumasta or Shop and Establishment licence?
A: It is the basic registration to run a shop, issued by the state or municipal authority, recording your business details and working hours. It is often needed to open a current account or get other licences.

Q: When do I need an FSSAI licence?
A: For any business that handles food — grocery, sweets, restaurants, packaged food. The level (basic registration, state or central licence) depends on turnover and scale, and the FSSAI number goes on bills and packaging.

Q: What is a trade licence?
A: A permission from the local municipal body to carry on a particular trade at a particular location, ensuring you follow local health and safety rules. Requirements vary by city and trade.

Q: Is GST registration a compliance requirement?
A: Yes, once turnover crosses the threshold, or immediately if you sell across states or online. Even below the threshold, voluntary registration lets you claim input tax credit.

Q: Do I need to renew licences?
A: Yes — renew before expiry and keep physical and digital copies handy. Lapsed registrations can mean fines, and banks and suppliers often ask to see current licences.

Q: Which registrations should I display?
A: Show your GSTIN on invoices and your FSSAI number on food bills. Visible compliance builds customer trust and signals a serious, lawful business.

Q: Do some businesses need special licences?
A: Yes — a pharmacy needs a drug licence, and shops using weighing scales may need weights-and-measures verification. Identify the specific licences for your line of business early.

Q: Is Udyam registration compulsory?
A: No, but it is free and valuable, unlocking cheaper loans, tender benefits and protection against delayed payments. Keep its details updated as the business grows.
