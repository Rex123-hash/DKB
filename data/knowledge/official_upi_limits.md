# UPI transaction limits and rules

For everyday use, UPI has a standard limit of ₹1 lakh per transaction and ₹1 lakh per day, set by NPCI. There is also a cap of about 20 UPI transactions per person per day. These limits cover almost all normal shop payments.

From September 2025, NPCI raised limits for certain categories. Verified merchants and specific sectors such as insurance, capital markets, travel, education and healthcare can accept higher amounts, with per-transaction limits up to ₹5 lakh and daily limits up to ₹10 lakh in some cases.

Banks and payment apps may set their own lower limits within the NPCI maximum, based on their risk rules. So the actual limit a shopkeeper sees can be lower than the NPCI cap, and it is worth checking with your own bank.

UPI is free for customers paying from a bank account, with no charge on normal person-to-merchant payments. This is why displaying a UPI QR code rarely loses a sale and speeds up collection.

For safety, remember that scanning a QR code only sends money, never receives it. Always confirm the money has reached your bank account before handing over goods, and never share an OTP or UPI PIN.

Source: National Payments Corporation of India (npci.org.in). Limits are revised from time to time, so verify the current limit with your bank.
