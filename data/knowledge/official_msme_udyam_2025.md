# MSME classification and Udyam registration (from April 2025)

From 1 April 2025 the MSME size limits were raised. A Micro enterprise is one with investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore. A Small enterprise has investment up to ₹25 crore and turnover up to ₹100 crore. A Medium enterprise has investment up to ₹125 crore and turnover up to ₹500 crore.

An enterprise must satisfy both the investment limit and the turnover limit for its category. If either one crosses the limit, the business moves up to the next category. Almost every local shop falls comfortably within the Micro category.

Udyam registration is the free, online registration for MSMEs on the government's Udyam portal. It needs only an Aadhaar and PAN, and there is no fee. It gives the business an official Udyam number.

The benefits of being Udyam-registered include easier access to collateral-free loans, lower interest rates, priority-sector lending, protection against delayed payments from buyers, and eligibility for various government subsidies and tenders.

Registering as an MSME does not add tax burden; it mainly unlocks credit and legal protections, so it is usually worth doing even for a small shop.

Source: Ministry of MSME (msme.gov.in), Udyam Registration portal (udyamregistration.gov.in), Union Budget 2025-26.
