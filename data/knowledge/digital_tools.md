# Going digital — apps and tools for shopkeepers

A digital khata app replaces the paper bahi-khata. It records credits and debits for each customer and supplier, shows running balances instantly, and can send automatic payment reminders. The big advantage is that nothing is lost or miscalculated, and you can check any account in seconds.

Billing and accounting apps create GST invoices, track sales and purchases, and produce monthly reports as PDFs. Many also manage stock and generate the figures needed for GST returns, turning hours of manual work into a few taps.

Inventory apps track stock levels and warn you before fast-moving items run out. Some let a supplier scan a QR code to add stock to your profile, which you approve before it is added — reducing manual entry and counting errors.

Digital payment records are a hidden benefit of going digital. Because UPI and card payments are logged automatically, your bookkeeping and tax filing become far easier, and you have verifiable proof of turnover for loans.

Start small when adopting digital tools: first move your udhaar khata to an app, then add billing, then stock. Trying to digitise everything at once is overwhelming; one habit at a time sticks better.

Keep your data backed up. A cloud-backed app protects your records even if the phone is lost or damaged, unlike a paper register that can be torn, wet or misplaced. Backup is one of the strongest reasons to go digital.

Use reminders and reports actively, not just for storage. Automatic payment reminders recover dues faster, and a weekly look at the sales and outstanding-dues report guides better buying and credit decisions.

Protect access to your business apps with a screen lock and a strong PIN or password, and never share OTPs. Your financial data is valuable, so treat app security as seriously as you treat the cash box.
